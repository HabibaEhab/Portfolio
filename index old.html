Schedify

MILPGASAAngular2025–2026Live Demo ↗
The Question

Basically: nobody wants to build a 1,470-person schedule by hand in Excel. So why were people still doing it?

The Challenge

Coverage rules, room capacity, people's preferences — all pulling in different directions. And it had to work for someone who's never seen an optimization model, not just for me.

The Process

Modeled it as a Mixed Integer Linear Program, then built a Genetic Algorithm and Simulated Annealing on top to see if they could keep up with an exact CBC solver.

The Solution

End result: a full web app — Flask on the backend, Angular on the front (that part was all me) — where you upload your data, hit run, and get a real schedule out.

The Outcome

Ranked one of the top 5 graduation projects in the department. I led the team of 5 that built it.

What I Learned

A perfect schedule is useless if nobody trusts it. Explaining the "why" mattered as much as getting the numbers right.
